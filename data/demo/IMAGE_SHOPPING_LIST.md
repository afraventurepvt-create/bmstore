# Image shopping list for the BMStore demo stores

One row per product. The **primary** file name is what the CSV `image_filename` column points to; shoot extra angles as `-2.jpg`, `-3.jpg` with the same stem (e.g. `ms-ss-001-…-2.jpg`). 75 primary images in total.

## Shooting / sourcing rules that apply to every photo

- **Format:** JPEG, sRGB, long edge at least 2000 px; crop the primary image to a 1:1 square (the storefront card) and keep a 4:5 version if you can (product page).
- **Backgrounds:** Meenakshi Silks on ivory or warm grey seamless paper; Anna Foods on a light wood or plain cream surface, top-down for packs; Sri Gold Jewels on black velvet (sets, necklaces) or ivory card (small items). Keep each store consistent so the grid looks like one shop.
- **Light:** window daylight or a single softbox from the side; no on-camera flash (kills zari and plating). Use a grey card once per session so colours are true; saree and stone colours sell the product.
- **Scale:** for small items (studs, toe rings, spice packs) include one frame with a coin, ruler or hand for scale.
- **People:** worn shots are optional and faces are never required (ear, wrist, waist, braid, shoulder crops are enough). If you photograph a person or a child, get written consent.
- **Sourcing instead of shooting:** only use images you have the rights to (your own, the supplier's with permission, or a licence). Do not scrape marketplace listings. Stock photos are acceptable as a stopgap for a demo only if the product shown matches the listing (same colour, same style).
- **Nothing to fake:** no overlaid badges, star ratings, 'bestseller' ribbons or 'X sold' stickers on images.
